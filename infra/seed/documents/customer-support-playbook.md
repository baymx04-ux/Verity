# Customer Support Playbook

## Channels
Support is available by email and in-app chat from 08:00 to 20:00 UTC on weekdays. Enterprise customers have a named contact and a shared escalation channel.

## First response targets
- SEV1 outage or data exposure: 30 minutes.
- SEV2 degraded service: 2 hours.
- SEV3 standard question: 4 hours.
- SEV4 enhancement request: 2 business days.

## Triage
Confirm the customer, plan, and affected workspace. Reproduce with the least privileged account that shows the issue. Collect timestamps, request IDs, and screenshots. Never ask a customer to share a password or full access token.

## Escalation
Escalate suspected security incidents to security@verity.dev immediately and do not speculate about cause. For billing disputes, open a finance ticket. For data deletion requests, follow the data retention policy and confirm identity before acting.

## Tone
Be direct, acknowledge impact, and avoid promising a fix date until engineering confirms it. Close the loop with the customer after the fix ships.
