# Employee Handbook

## Welcome
Verity is a remote-first company. This handbook summarizes the expectations, benefits, and processes that apply to every employee. It is a living document; the People team publishes changes through the company wiki and email.

## Working hours
Employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside those hours, schedules are flexible. Managers and teams should document any on-call or customer-facing coverage requirements.

## Conduct
Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated. Raise concerns with your manager, the People team, or the confidential ethics line. Reports are investigated promptly and documented.

## Security
Use the company password manager and enable multi-factor authentication on every work account. Keep laptops encrypted and patched. Do not store customer data on personal devices. Report suspected security incidents to security@verity.dev immediately.

## Time off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off each year plus local public holidays. Request time off at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Sick leave is separate and does not require advance notice.

## Expenses
Follow the travel and expense policies. Submit receipts within thirty days. Managers approve expenses in the finance system.
