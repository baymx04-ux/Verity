# Benefits Summary

## Health

Full-time employees and dependents are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on the first day of the month after hire. Verity covers 90 percent of employee premiums and 70 percent of dependent premiums.

## Retirement

Verity matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 4 percent of salary. Employees are vested immediately. Contribution changes can be made monthly.

## Parental leave

All new parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave, regardless of gender or path to parenthood. Leave may be taken in blocks within the first year. Adoption and surrogacy support up to 10,000 USD.

## Wellbeing

The annual learning budget is 1,500 USD. The home-office stipend is 500 USD. Employees also receive a 100 USD monthly wellbeing allowance for gym, therapy, or childcare support.

## Time off

25 days of paid time off, local public holidays, and separate sick leave. Benefits questions go to people@verity.dev. This summary is not a substitute for the official plan documents.
