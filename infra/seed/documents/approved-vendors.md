## Offboarding

When a vendor is terminated, revoke SSO access, rotate any shared credentials, export required records, and confirm deletion in writing. Finance updates the vendor register within five business days.

## Review cadence

Critical vendors are reviewed annually. High-risk findings require remediation within thirty days. Questions go to procurement@verity.dev.
