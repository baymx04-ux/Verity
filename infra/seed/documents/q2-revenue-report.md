# Q2 FY2026 Revenue Report

> Historical document. Q2 figures are superseded by the Q3 revenue report for current planning.

## Headline

Q2 revenue was 9.8 million USD, up 11 percent quarter over quarter. Annual recurring revenue reached 41.6 million USD. Net revenue retention was 108 percent.

## Segments

Enterprise contributed 58 percent of revenue, mid-market 29 percent, and self-serve 13 percent. Gross revenue churn was 5.0 percent. The sales team closed 14 new enterprise logos.

## Priorities

The quarter focused on onboarding, admin permissions, and reliability. Support volume per customer fell 7 percent after the knowledge base launch. Hiring was paused for two weeks while the go-to-market plan was revised.
