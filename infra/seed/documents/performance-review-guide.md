# Performance Review Guide

## Cadence

Verity runs reviews twice a year, in April and October. Reviews cover impact, craft, collaboration, and values. Employees write a self-review, collect peer feedback, and meet with their manager.

## Ratings

Ratings are not forced into a distribution. Managers calibrate across teams to keep expectations consistent. A rating summarizes sustained performance; it is not a prediction of promotion.

## Feedback

Feedback should be specific, timely, and tied to observable behavior. Use the situation-behavior-impact format. Avoid surprises: managers share concerns during regular one-to-ones, not for the first time in a review.

## Promotion

Promotion requires sustained performance at the next level, not a single project. The manager prepares a packet with evidence and a peer panel reviews it. Timelines vary by role and available scope.

## Development

Every employee should have one growth goal and one skill goal per half. Use the learning budget for courses or coaching. HR keeps review records for the period required by local law.
