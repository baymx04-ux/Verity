# Travel Policy

## Flights

Employees may book economy travel for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than six hours when the trip is approved by a director. Business class requires vice-president approval and must be booked at least fourteen days in advance unless an exception is documented.

## Hotels

Hotel rates are capped at 250 USD per night in standard cities and 400 USD per night in high-cost cities. Book through the corporate travel portal when possible. Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD.

## Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to 75 USD per day, excluding client entertainment. Alcohol is reimbursed only when it is part of an approved client dinner and is itemized separately.

## Reporting

Expense reports are due within thirty days of the trip. Late reports require manager approval and may be reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Personal travel combined with a business trip must be clearly separated on the expense report. Unused tickets must be cancelled or rebooked within the airline's rules.
