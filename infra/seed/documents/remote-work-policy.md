# Remote Work Policy

Verity is remote-first. Employees may work from home, a coworking space, or another location that meets the security requirements below.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible. Contractors follow the terms in their agreement. Customer-facing roles must maintain coverage during their published support hours.

## Workspace

Verity provides a one-time 500 USD home-office stipend for a desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment. Keep receipts. The stipend is prorated for employees who join after July 1.

## Security requirements

Use company-managed laptops with full-disk encryption. Connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems from a public network. Never use public computers for customer data. Lock your screen when stepping away and keep software updates enabled.

## Working across time zones

Teams should record meetings when practical and share written summaries. Avoid recurring meetings outside an employee's core hours without agreement. Travel to company gatherings is covered by the travel policy.
