# Pricing Guide

## Current plans
- Starter: 300 USD per month, up to 10 users.
- Team: 1,200 USD per month, up to 50 users, includes SSO and audit export.
- Enterprise: 2,500 USD per month starting January 2026, custom retention and support.

## Legacy pricing
Older sales decks list Enterprise at 2,000 USD per month. That price is outdated and must not be quoted for new contracts. Existing customers on the legacy price keep it until renewal unless a new agreement is signed.

## Discounting
Sales may discount up to 10 percent without approval. Discounts from 10 to 20 percent require the sales director. Anything above 20 percent requires the CFO and must include a business case.

## Packaging rules
Do not unbundle SSO or audit export from Team and Enterprise. Usage overages are billed monthly. All prices are in USD and exclude taxes.

## Questions
Route pricing exceptions to finance@verity.dev. Quote only from the current price book in the sales portal.
