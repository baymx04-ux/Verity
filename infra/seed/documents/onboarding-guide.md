# Onboarding Guide

## Before day one
People operations ships the laptop, signs you into the identity provider, and invites you to your team calendar. Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy and a first-week schedule.

## First week
Complete security training, set up multi-factor authentication, and install the password manager. Meet your team and read the handbook, access policy, and remote work policy. Ship one small change or documentation update so you learn the workflow.

## First month
Agree on 30-day goals with your manager. Shadow a customer conversation or support rotation where relevant. Confirm your home-office stipend and benefits enrollment.

## First 90 days
Review the 30/60/90 plan, gather feedback from peers, and identify one process improvement. Your manager documents progress in the performance system. Ask for feedback early; the goal is clarity, not a surprise review.

## Where to ask
Use the help channel for tooling, the People team for benefits, and security@verity.dev for anything involving credentials or customer data.
