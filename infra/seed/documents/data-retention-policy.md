# Data Retention Policy

## Records

Financial records are retained for seven years. Signed contracts are retained for the life of the agreement plus seven years. Support transcripts are retained for two years. Access logs are retained for one year unless an investigation requires longer retention.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are retained for 35 days. Restoring a backup requires approval from the security lead. Backup copies follow the same access rules as production data.

## Deletion

When a document is deleted, the original file, indexed chunks, and citations are removed. Deletion is immediate in production and completes in backups within the backup window. Personal data deletion requests are verified and logged.

## Legal holds

A legal hold suspends deletion for named matters. Employees must not delete records covered by a hold. The legal team issues and releases holds in writing.

## Review

The security lead reviews retention schedules annually with finance, support, and legal. Changes are recorded in the audit log.
