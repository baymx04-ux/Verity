# Security Baseline

## Identity

All workforce accounts use single sign-on. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, source control, cloud consoles, and the password manager. Shared accounts are prohibited. Access follows least privilege and is reviewed quarterly.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption, automatic updates, endpoint protection, and a screen lock after five minutes. Personal devices may access email only through the managed profile. Jailbroken or rooted devices are not permitted.

## Data handling

Classify data as public, internal, confidential, or restricted. Customer data may only live in approved systems. Do not paste confidential data into unapproved AI tools or public issue trackers. Encrypt exports and delete them when the task is complete.

## Vendors

Vendors that process customer data complete an annual security review covering access control, encryption, incident notification, and subprocessors. Critical findings must be remediated within thirty days.

## Reporting

Report phishing, lost devices, and suspected exposure to security@verity.dev. Do not investigate alone or delete evidence. The incident response policy defines severity and notification timelines.
