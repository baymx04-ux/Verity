# Workforce Plan FY2027

CONFIDENTIAL - PEOPLE GROUP ONLY

## Hiring
The approved plan adds 14 roles: 6 engineering, 3 product, 2 sales, 2 support, and 1 finance. Backfills require vice-president approval. Contractors may be used for short-term data migrations but not for customer-facing support without security review.

## Reduction scenario
If revenue misses the Q4 plan by more than 15 percent, leadership will evaluate a reduction of up to 8 percent of non-sales roles. No decision has been made. Any reduction must follow local consultation requirements and be communicated by the CEO and People lead.

## Compensation
The merit pool is 4 percent for FY2027. Promotion increases are funded separately. Equity refresh grants are reserved for critical retention cases and require a calibration panel.

Do not share this document outside the people group. Do not paste it into general channels or external tools. Workforce planning discussions are confidential until formally announced.
