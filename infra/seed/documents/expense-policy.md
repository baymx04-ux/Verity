# Expense Policy

## General rules
Spend company money as if it were your own. Every expense must have a business purpose and an itemized receipt. Submit expenses within thirty days of the purchase. Missing receipts above 25 USD require manager approval and a written explanation.

## Limits
- Meals during local work: 30 USD per person.
- Client meals: 100 USD per person with attendees listed.
- Software subscriptions: require manager approval and a security review for customer-data access.
- Equipment: follow the home-office stipend and procurement policy.

## Approvals
Expenses under 500 USD are approved by the direct manager. Expenses from 500 to 5,000 USD require director approval. Anything above 5,000 USD requires finance and a vice-president. Splitting a purchase to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.

## Corporate cards
Use the corporate card for travel and approved software. Personal cards may be used for emergencies and are reimbursed through payroll. Lost cards must be reported to finance the same day.
