# Q3 FY2026 Revenue Report

## Headline

Q3 revenue was 12.4 million USD, up 18 percent quarter over quarter. Annual recurring revenue reached 49.1 million USD. Net revenue retention was 112 percent.

## Segments

Enterprise contributed 61 percent of revenue, mid-market 27 percent, and self-serve 12 percent. Enterprise average contract value rose to 84,000 USD. Self-serve conversion improved by 2.1 points after the onboarding redesign.

## Churn and pipeline

Gross revenue churn was 4.2 percent, down from 5.0 percent in Q2. The pipeline entering Q4 is 16.8 million USD, with 38 percent in late-stage negotiation. Two large renewals are at risk due to security review delays.

## Guidance

Q4 guidance is 13.6 to 14.0 million USD. The plan assumes no major acquisition and includes a 1.1 million USD investment in retrieval infrastructure. This report supersedes the Q2 numbers for any forward-looking discussion.
